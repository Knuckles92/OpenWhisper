## Lifecycle fixes

- Keep completed minute windows queued when the worker is busy; do not jump over earlier windows when transcription advances.
- Use committed audio progress to close a window even if its final chunk is silent.
- Recheck an earlier window when late-channel speech or a rolling revision arrives; suppress unchanged successful checks.
- Replace a rechecked minute's pulses so invalidated highlights do not linger.
- After transcript cleanup and report generation, independently run a final highlight pass, including short meetings and the final partial minute. This does not require the notes agent.
